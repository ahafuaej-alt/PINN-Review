### Analytical governance

Task 6.1 formalizes 15 Level-6 invariants, including:

- frequency is not evidence strength;
- repeated statements/citations within one paper are not independent support;
- contradiction/mismatch/ambiguity/negative evidence remain visible;
- qualitative association is not effect size or causality;
- all material aggregates must declare numerator, denominator, analytical unit, eligibility filter, scope and ontology/normalization version;
- paper, record, assignment, result, relation, evidence, collaboration-edge and independent-study/PEU counts remain distinct;
- the global corpus size is not an automatic denominator for every dimension;
- L6 analytical signals cannot silently become official L7 synthesis;
- Task 5.5 remains the ontology-promotion authority.
