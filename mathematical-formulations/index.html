Direct

Equivalent
An evidence source uses an equivalent or application-specific form normalized to Atlas notation.
Synthesized
A mathematically conservative cross-source generalization, never presented as a verbatim equation from one paper.

How to read the catalogue

One mathematical narrative, not an equation list

The logical stream begins with the governing problem, replaces the unknown field by a trainable representation, applies physics to that representation, and turns the resulting requirement into a trainable objective. It then branches according to what changes: enforcement mechanism, state variables, numerical operator, domain organization, approximation target, uncertainty model, training dynamics, or validation criterion.
