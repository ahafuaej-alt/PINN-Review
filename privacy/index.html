Atlas / Privacy

Privacy by design

Transparent measurement,
without visitor profiles.

The PINN Review Atlas uses privacy-preserving, aggregated analytics to understand its research reach. It does not use accounts, advertising, analytics cookies, contact forms, or cross-site visitor profiles.

Aggregated reach statistics

The Atlas uses GoatCounter to count visits and summarize approximate country-level reach. GoatCounter processes standard request information, including the page path, referrer, IP address, user agent, language, and screen width, to produce aggregate statistics. Source IP addresses and user-agent strings are not retained in its statistics database.

The homepage publishes only total visits, visits during the previous 30 days, the number of countries reached, and the five leading country totals. It never publishes an individual visit or visitor record. Collection starts on the date shown in the reach panel; earlier traffic is not reconstructed.

Data stored on your device

The site uses browser local storage for three optional conveniences:

  • Light, dark, or system theme choice
  • Selected-reference reading list
  • Recent bibliography searches

This information stays in your browser and can be removed by clearing this site’s storage.

No Atlas accounts or profiles

The Atlas has no user accounts, login system, first-party server session, or profile database. GoatCounter estimates visits without analytics cookies and does not build cross-site profiles.

External destinations

DOI, publisher, GitHub, and other external links open third-party websites. Their own privacy and cookie policies apply after you follow those links.

Hosting and transport

The public site is served by GitHub Pages over HTTPS. GitHub may process standard infrastructure logs under its own service policies. When aggregated analytics is active, the browser also contacts GoatCounter over HTTPS to record the visit.

Downloads and clipboard

Citation files are generated locally in the browser. Copy actions use the browser clipboard only after you press the relevant button.

Corrections and contact

Reference corrections are reported through the public repository’s structured GitHub issue form; no personal contact form is embedded in the Atlas.
