RESEARCH POLICY
- Each missing Atlas ID was matched to its target publication by title and DOI.
- A named activation function was added only when the target publication or a directly
  corresponding target-paper record explicitly exposed it.
- Activation functions were not inferred from related papers, cited papers, standard
  architecture defaults, or common practice.
- "N/A" therefore means that the source classification or accessible target-paper
  evidence does not support a named activation function. The accompanying note preserves
  the reason.
